# Duck sounds

The quacks (`quack01.wav` … `quack12.wav`) are short excerpts cut, filtered
and pitch-shifted from these recordings on Wikimedia Commons. They are shared
under the same Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike licences:

- "Domestic duck sound 01" by Ganesh Mohan T, CC BY-SA 4.0 —
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Domestic_duck_sound_01.wav
- "Anas platyrhynchos - Mallard - XC62258" by Jonathon Jongsma (xeno-canto),
  CC BY-SA 3.0 — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anas_platyrhynchos_-_Mallard_-_XC62258.ogg
- "Pekin duck & mallard" by WaderClub, CC BY-SA 4.0 —
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pekin_duck_%26_mallard.ogg
  (downloaded and analysed; none of its quacks made the gentle cut)

The duckling peeps (`peep1.wav` … `peep4.wav`) are synthesised.
